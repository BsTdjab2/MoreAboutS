Educational Purpose

This platform and its associated community are dedicated exclusively to educational research in firmware development, embedded hardware, and ethical security testing. Nothing published here constitutes an authorization or encouragement to target systems without prior ownership or explicit consent.

Hardware Risk

Flashing custom firmware carries inherent hardware risks, including permanent device bricking. Hardware variations, bootloader nuances, or faulty cabling can interrupt write operations. Ensure you understand force-download recovery procedures and stock firmware reflashing before attempting any modifications. Assume full technical risk for device recovery.

Legal Compliance

While wireless testing hardware is generally legal to possess, deploying these tools against unauthorized networks violates cybercrime laws in most jurisdictions. Unauthorized RF transmission, client deauthentication, or packet capture carries severe legal penalties. Always secure explicit, written authorization prior to testing non-owned infrastructure.
