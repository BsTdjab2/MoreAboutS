03 / CONCEPT Planned idea

Account Security Guard

A defensive tool concept that helps protect an account or email when suspicious access is detected. It could alert the owner, help review and revoke unknown sessions where supported, and guide the owner through official password and recovery-contact updates. Sensitive changes should remain under the account owner's control.

04 / CONCEPT Planned idea

Instagram Ad Filter

A tool concept to reduce repetitive and unwanted Instagram advertisements by helping identify and hide promoted content where platform and browser capabilities allow. Its technical feasibility and compatibility with Instagram's rules would need to be checked before development.

This page is a project tracker, not a promise that every planned concept is already working. Ideas may change as they are researched and developed.
