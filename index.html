Web Flasher ↓

Plug an ESP32 or ESP8266 board into a USB port, pick a firmware and your board (or bring your own .bin), and flash it — no install, no account.

The flasher needs the Web Serial API, which means desktop Chrome, Edge or Opera. Firefox, Safari and every iOS browser don't support it yet.
