Privacy_

What this site collects, and what it doesn't.

Overview

This is a personal, non-commercial website with a strict privacy-first policy: no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising frameworks, and no cookies. The only data processed is what you voluntarily submit via the contact form.

Contact Form Data Collection

When you submit a message, the following information is transmitted: your provided name, email address, message body, and any optional file attachments. Your IP address is temporarily processed by the relay service strictly for rate-limiting and anti-abuse enforcement, and is never stored alongside your message.

Messages are routed through a custom Cloudflare Workers relay into a private, secured Discord channel. Cloudflare and Discord process this data during transmission according to their respective privacy policies. Messages remain in this channel until manually purged.

Web Flasher & Client-Side Execution

Zero data is collected or uploaded. Selected firmware binaries are downloaded directly within your browser, and local files are read entirely client-side. Hardware flashing occurs locally over USB via the Web Serial API, restricted strictly to the hardware port you explicitly authorize in the browser prompt.

Local Storage

A single entry is stored in your browser's localStorage solely to persist your preferred color theme across visits. It contains no tracking identifiers, never leaves your local browser, and is completely erased if you clear your site data.

Third-Party Services

  • The site is served strictly as static files from its primary host.
  • Contact messages route securely through Cloudflare Workers and deliver directly to Discord.
  • Firmware downloads, when configured, are fetched directly from their respective publishing repositories (exposing your IP address as any standard web request would).

No external fonts, third-party scripts, or CDN-hosted assets are embedded. All required resources are self-hosted directly from this domain.

User Control & Rights

If you prefer not to submit details through the web form, you are encouraged to connect directly via Discord. To request the deletion of a previously sent message, contact us and it will be permanently removed. There are no user accounts to manage and no mailing lists to unsubscribe from.

Children's Privacy

This platform is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from minors.

Policy Updates

Any modifications to this privacy policy will be reflected by an updated revision date at the top of this page.
