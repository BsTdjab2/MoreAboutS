Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026.

Summary

This is a personal, non-commercial site. It has no accounts, no forms, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. I do not collect or sell personal information through it.

What is stored in your browser

If you switch between light and dark mode, the site saves that choice in your browser's local storage under a single key so it is remembered next time. It holds no identifier, is never sent anywhere, and disappears when you clear your site data.

Hosting logs

The site is served as static files by a hosting provider. Like any web host, it may record technical request data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. I do not have access to a profile of you from this and I do not use it to identify visitors.

Contact

The contact buttons open Discord, GitHub, YouTube or your email app. If you write to me, I receive whatever you choose to send, such as your email address and message, and I use it only to reply. Those platforms handle your data under their own privacy policies. You can ask me to delete a conversation at any time by writing to lxveace@proton.me.

Outbound links

Links to other sites are not covered by this policy. Once you follow one, the other site's rules apply.

Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.
